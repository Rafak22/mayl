# Meeting notes (forecasting project)

## Kickoff meeting (May 2026)
The client promised its store managers that order forecasts are in the ERP by 11:00 every day, because supplier orders close at 12:00. Agreed to start with a global LightGBM model on 3 years of sales history.

## Category manager review (Oct 2026)
In the first week of last Ramadan, forecasts for dates and laban were about 35% too low, and several stores ran out. The category managers asked that forecasts for Ramadan and Eid weeks are reviewed and signed off by them before orders go out.

## Decision pending
The data scientists want to try a deep learning model for better accuracy. The engineers worry about the nightly batch window.
