# Category rules (from the fresh-food category managers)

## Staple items
Bread, milk, laban and eggs are staples. Never order a staple below last week's minimum daily sales for that store. An empty staple shelf costs more customer trust than the waste it saves.

## Ramadan and Eid
Forecasts for Ramadan and Eid weeks must be reviewed and signed off by the category managers at least 2 weeks before. Dates, laban, sambousa pastry and juices jump sharply in the first Ramadan week.

## Salary day
Demand rises by about 20% on the 27th of each month (salary day) and the two days after.

## New stores
For the first 8 weeks after opening, a new store uses the sales history of a similar "sister" store instead of its own.

## Shelf life
Bread: 1 day. Fresh dairy: 7 days. Produce: 3 to 5 days. Fresh meat: 2 days.
