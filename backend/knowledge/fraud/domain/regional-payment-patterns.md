# Regional payment patterns (fraud domain guide)

## Family transfers
Transfers between family members are very common in the region, especially at month start (salary day) and before Eid and Ramadan. Typical amounts are SAR 500 to SAR 15,000. These transfers repeat to the same receivers over months and are rarely fraud.

## Known-contact signals
A receiver who has received money from the same sender 3 or more times in the last 6 months is a known contact. Known-contact transfers have a fraud rate below 0.02%.

## Large transfers
Transfers above SAR 50,000 must always be reviewed by an analyst, regardless of relationship, because of anti-money-laundering obligations. Never auto-clear these.

## Mule account patterns
Real fraud often moves money through a chain of new accounts within minutes ("money mules"). Signals: receiver account younger than 30 days, many inbound transfers from unrelated senders, immediate outbound transfer.
