# Kickoff meeting notes (fraud project)

## Client goals
The bank wants to catch fraud in near real time and cut analyst workload. Analysts currently review too many false alarms, especially around salary day.

## Promises made
We agreed to flag every transfer above SAR 50,000 for analyst review. Pilot in 4 weeks.

## Decisions
Use XGBoost for explainability. Revisit deep models after the pilot.
