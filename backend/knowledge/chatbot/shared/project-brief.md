# Project brief: Sahm Telecom Arabic customer-service chatbot

## Client and goal
Sahm Telecom (synthetic client) serves 4 million prepaid and postpaid customers in Saudi Arabia. The chatbot answers customer questions in Arabic and English inside the app and WhatsApp, and hands over to a human agent when needed. Goal: resolve routine questions without waiting for an agent.

## Success metrics agreed with the client
Answer accuracy at least 90% on the monthly evaluation set. At least 60% of chats resolved without a human (containment). Handover to a human in under 30 seconds when the customer asks for one or the topic is sensitive.

## Status
Live since August 2026. Current results: accuracy 91%, containment 64%, median handover 22 seconds. No open issues.
