# Sample patient messages with expected outcomes (synthetic)

## Emergency examples
"عندي ألم شديد في صدري وضيق نفس" (severe chest pain and shortness of breath) → emergency advice + nurse alert. "My father's face is drooping and he can't lift his arm" → emergency advice + nurse alert.

## Nurse callback examples
"ولدي عمره سنة وحرارته ٣٨ ونص" (my son is one year old with a temperature of 38.5) → nurse callback (child under 2). "I'm 20 weeks pregnant and have some bleeding" → nurse callback.

## Same-day examples
"عندي حرارة من يومين وألم في الأذن" (fever for two days and ear pain) → same-day appointment. "Burning when I urinate since yesterday" → same-day appointment.

## Routine examples
"ابي أجدد وصفة الضغط" (I want to renew my blood pressure prescription) → routine booking. "Can I book my daughter's vaccination next week?" → routine booking.

## Mixed messages
"عندي حرارة من ثلاث أيام ابي موعد" (fever for three days, I want an appointment) → same-day appointment, unless the fever is above 39°C, then nurse callback.
