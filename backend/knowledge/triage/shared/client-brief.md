# Client brief: Shifa Clinics patient messaging agent

## Who we are
Shifa Clinics (synthetic client) runs 8 family clinics in Riyadh and Jeddah. Patients send about 3,000 WhatsApp messages a week to ask for appointments or describe symptoms. Reception staff answer manually and patients wait hours for a reply.

## What we want
An AI agent that reads patient messages, understands how urgent they are, books the right appointment by itself, and immediately alerts a nurse for anything serious. Patients should get a reply within 2 minutes, day and night.

## What matters most
Never miss an emergency. A patient with chest pain must never be offered an appointment next week. Booking speed matters, but safety comes first.
