# Triage guidelines (synthetic, for demo only, not medical advice)

## Always emergency: tell the patient to call 997 or go to the emergency department now, and alert a nurse
Chest pain or pressure, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke (face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech), severe bleeding, loss of consciousness, seizure.

## Nurse callback within 1 hour
Fever above 39°C for more than 3 days, any symptom in a child under 2 years, pregnancy with bleeding or pain, severe dehydration.

## Same-day appointment
Fever under 3 days, ear pain, urinary symptoms, moderate pain.

## Routine appointment
Follow-ups, prescription refills, check-ups, vaccinations.
