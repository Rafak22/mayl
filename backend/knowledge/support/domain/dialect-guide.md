# Arabic support tickets: dialect guide

## Gulf dialect
Most tickets are written in Gulf dialect mixed with Modern Standard Arabic and English words. Common examples: "ابي" (I want), "وش" (what), "الحين" (now). The classifier must handle these.

## Escalation words
Tickets mentioning legal action, regulators, or "شكوى رسمية" (formal complaint) must always go to the Complaints team.
