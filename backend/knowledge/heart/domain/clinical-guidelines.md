# Clinical guidelines for wearable heart readings (synthetic)

## Resting heart rate
Normal adult resting heart rate is 60 to 100 bpm. Readings above 120 bpm at rest or below 40 bpm should be reviewed by a nurse.

## Exercise
High heart rate during physical activity is normal. Readings taken during detected activity (step count high in the previous 10 minutes) should not be flagged on heart rate alone.

## Irregular rhythm
An irregular rhythm notification from the device should always be reviewed, even if the heart rate is normal.

## Sensor errors
Single isolated readings above 220 bpm are almost always sensor errors.
