# Client brief (heart project)

## Goal
14 clinics want to flag abnormal heart readings from patients' wearables so nurses can call patients early. Missing a real case is much worse than a false alarm.

## Constraints
Results within 5 minutes. Patient data stays in-country. Pilot in 6 weeks across 3 clinics.

## Open question
The cardiologist said the team still needs to agree on what counts as abnormal.
