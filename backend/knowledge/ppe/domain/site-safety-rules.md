# Site safety rules (from the HSE safety officer)

## Helmet rule
A helmet counts only when it is worn on the head with the strap fastened where visible. A helmet carried in the hand, clipped to a belt, hanging on equipment or lying on the ground is a violation.

## Exemptions
Workers inside closed site offices and vehicle cabins are exempt. Camera zones covering offices are marked and must not raise alerts.

## Alert priority
High priority: no helmet in active work zones (under cranes, at height, near heavy machinery). Normal priority: everywhere else on site.

## Alert fatigue
If a safety officer receives more than about 10 false alerts per camera per day, they start ignoring alerts. Keeping false alerts low is part of safety.
