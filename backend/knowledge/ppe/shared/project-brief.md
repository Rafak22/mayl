# Project brief: helmet compliance detection for construction sites

## Client and goal
Binaa Contracting (synthetic client) runs 6 large construction sites in Riyadh and NEOM-area projects. Fixed site cameras send an image every 30 seconds. The system detects workers without a helmet and alerts the site safety officer, so violations are fixed before an accident.

## Success metrics agreed with the client
Recall at least 95% for "worker without helmet" (missing a violation is the costly error). False alerts under 10 per camera per day, otherwise safety officers stop trusting alerts. Alert within 60 seconds of the image.

## Status
Mid-project. Model v3 is trained and the alert engine is being built. Pilot on 2 sites in 3 weeks.
