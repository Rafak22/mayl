# Meeting notes (PPE project)

## Kickoff meeting (Sep 2026)
The client promised its board a pilot on 2 sites in 3 weeks from the final model sign-off. We agreed to use a public-domain hard hat dataset plus 1,200 of the client's own site images.

## Site visit with the safety officer (Oct 2026)
The safety officer reviewed 50 alerts. Main problem: many workers counted as "compliant" were only carrying a helmet in their hand or had it clipped to their belt. Under the site rules, that is a violation. He asked that only a helmet worn on the head counts.
